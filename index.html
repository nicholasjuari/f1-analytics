Car vs Driver — The Evidence
When the car wins
In 2023, Red Bull won 21 of 22 races with two different drivers. This strongly suggests the car — not Verstappen alone — was responsible for the bulk of that dominance.
When the driver wins
Senna won 5 races in 1993 in a Ford-powered McLaren against the dominant Williams-Renault — widely considered the greatest single season of driving in F1 history.
